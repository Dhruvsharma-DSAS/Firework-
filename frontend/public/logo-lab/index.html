01

Scan Globe

From your first image: a blue globe, a satellite top-right with an orange body, a white scan beam hitting the Earth and a pixelated fire patch spreading from that point, wrapped by an orbit swoosh. Kept: the beam-to-pixel story — it says exactly what the site does. Changed for our site: the orbit swoosh is VIIRS cyan instead of navy, the satellite body is MODIS amber, the outline is soft ink so it reads on our black, and the fire pixels use the same heat scale as the Explorer globe.

Animated · on site background

Motion: the orbit swoosh draws in, then the fire pixels light up outward from the beam point like a detection spreading; the beam pulses, a ring ripples at the hit point, a cyan dot rides the orbit and the continents drift slowly as the Earth turns. Satellite bobs gently.

Mission Patch

From your second image: a round NASA-style patch with a white and amber double ring, a blue-to-red gauge arc across the top, a silver satellite beaming down onto Earth's night limb with city lights, a pixel fire grid where the beam lands, an amber orbit swoosh and three stacked data layers (fire, land, cloud). Kept: the gauge (it reads as our 2000→2026 time / heat scale) and the stacked layers — they match the Explorer's context layers exactly. Changed: colours locked to our palette, cleaner geometry so it survives at 32 px.

Animated · on site background

Motion: gauge segments light up one by one from cool blue to fire red, then reset — a 25-year record filling in. The beam and lens pulse, fire pixels ignite from the beam point, stars twinkle, city lights flicker, the three data layers float in a stagger and the orbit draws in.

03

Fire Ribbon

From your third image: a navy globe with cream continents, wrapped by a white-edged ribbon of pixels that runs from cool blue to hot orange, sweeping down into a second orange band with a forest silhouette at the bottom. Kept: the ribbon is the strongest idea here — it is literally the record (cool past to hot present, made of pixels) wrapping the planet. Changed: pixel colours run through our VIIRS cyan → MODIS amber → fire red, the trees sit on a glowing fire band so the story ends in fire, edges are crisp for small sizes.

Animated · on site background

Motion: the outer arc draws, then the pixel ribbon unrolls from left to right across the globe; a heat wave keeps travelling along it from blue to orange, the fire glow behind the trees breathes like flames, and the continents drift as the globe turns.
